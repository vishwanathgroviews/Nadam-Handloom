How to review

Four steps

Open the Design options panel (bottom right) at any time. Your changes stay in this browser until you press Reset demo.

Find things

Search "300k" or a price like "2800". Try the filters Hidden, Needs photo and No products.

Change a price

Open Rainbow checks sarees → Edit, type 54310 as the store price and Save: see the price check.

Hide and delete

Delete an empty subcategory: it disappears. Try deleting one with stock: refused with the count.

Send a PDF

Make a catalogue PDF and share it on WhatsApp. Switch Android / iPhone in Design options.

What this mock-up follows

  • Delete never erases. It sets the item inactive: gone from the app and the website, kept for old bills and history.
  • No delete while any saree is in stock; a category only after all its subcategories are deleted; deleted names can be reused.
  • Hide from website is separate from delete: hidden items stay in the app.
  • MRP field with the same "% off" the website shows; MRP must be above the website price.
  • Price check: old → new before saving, warning when a price more than doubles, halves, or goes under ₹100.
  • Search across every category; filters; drag to reorder; price history; photo while creating, with crop.
  • Catalogue PDF straight to the customer's WhatsApp on Android; share menu on iPhone; Owners and Staff.
Fixed from the old app

Content and behaviour fixes

  • 149 subcategories had an MRP the app couldn't show or change.
  • Delete erased the subcategory, its products and some customer orders.
  • A real price went from ₹3,800 to ₹54,310 with no check.
  • Renaming a category broke its web address; duplicate names were allowed.
  • "N pieces" label for products, and the store price shown twice.
  • PDF screen said "every active product"; it now says exactly who is included.
