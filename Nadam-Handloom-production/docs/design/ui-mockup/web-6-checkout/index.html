Checkout and payment

From the bag to a paid order: sign in, choose the address, review, pay with Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets), and the confirmation. Every other ending too: payment failed, window closed, bank slow, the 15-minute hold ending, a saree selling meanwhile. Built in the live website's own look, with real sarees and prices.

How to use it

  1. Start from the bag and press Checkout. Sign in (a shortcut), choose an address, review, press Pay.
  2. Razorpay's window is a stand-in drawn by us: its yellow box lets you choose what happens (succeeds, fails, bank slow, closed).
  3. Press Design options (bottom left) to try the lettered choices; A is my recommendation. Screen states there jumps to every ending.
  4. Press Copy my choices and send them. Nothing is built before your picks.

What the old checkout got wrong (fixed in W6)

  • Any payment was marked paid, even for an order cancelled at the counter or paid after the hold: a saree could sell twice. Now a late payment goes through only if every saree is still there, otherwise it is refunded automatically.
  • The browser's word alone was trusted. Now the server also asks Razorpay that the money arrived, for this amount.
  • Paying again made a new order that blocked the customer's own sarees. Now "Pay now" reuses the order and its hold.
  • "Order Confirmed!" showed even for a cancelled order. Now each ending has its own page.
  • Razorpay's notices go to the old server. They move to the new one at go-live.
