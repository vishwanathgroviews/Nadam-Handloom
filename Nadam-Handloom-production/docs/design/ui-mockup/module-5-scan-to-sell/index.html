How to review

Four things to try

Open the Design options panel (bottom right) at any time. Your changes stay in this browser until you press Reset demo.

Make a bill

Start selling → press "Saree in stock" two or three times in the pretend-scan bar → View bill → type a bargain price.

Complete it

Complete sale → At the counter, Cash, Invoice yes → Share on WhatsApp → View invoice (GST split).

Hit a problem

Press "Sold saree", "Held online", "Online only" while scanning. Try a price under half the store price.

Undo a mistake

Today's sales → open a sale → Cancel sale (owners). Switch to Ravi · Staff to see the difference.

A quick "Is this price right?" when an agreed price is under half the store price. Note: today 1 in 4 bargains is under half.

No selling offline

Scanning and the bill work offline; Complete sale waits for the internet, so a saree can never be sold twice.

Fixed from the old app

Behaviour fixes

  • Price check no longer wipes the bill; the bill is kept on the phone.
  • A slow network or a double tap can't sell twice or show a false "already sold".
  • "Sell anyway" for a saree held online now cancels that online checkout (no double sale).
  • Payment mode recorded; sales can be cancelled (owners, same day).
  • Online only, switched-off and damaged sarees aren't sold at the counter.
  • A failed invoice can be made again; WhatsApp orders carry the customer's name.
  • Errors say which saree on the bill was the problem.
