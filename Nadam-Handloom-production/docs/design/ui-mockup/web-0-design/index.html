Try sign-in
Mobile number

One screen for sign-in and sign-up. Type the mobile, tap Send code. A hidden trap field stops simple bots.

SMS code

6 boxes; Android fills it in by itself. Valid 10 minutes, 3 tries, resend after 60 seconds, 5 codes an hour per number.

New customer: profile

Full name, email and pincode; city and state fill in from the pincode. Optional tick for offers. Once only.

My account

Picture, profile, change mobile (code on the new number), addresses with landmark, notifications (Firebase), devices, delete account with 7 days to cancel.

The three designs

Each is a complete look: colours, fonts, the top menu, the home banner, the saree cards and motion. You can still mix parts in Design options (bottom left of every page).

The premium home page

Built like a luxury house

The home page follows the big saree houses: one story told from top to bottom, large photos, few words and motion that follows your scroll.

Opening

A short logo reveal, then a full-screen cinematic opening led by the weaver at the loom, with five chapters (Loom, Zari, Pastels, Kalamkari, Everyday).

Statement and collections

A brand line that lights up word by word as you scroll, then an editorial photo grid that wipes in: sarees, dress materials, pure pattu, all collections.

Sarees and edits

New arrivals framed like gallery pieces in a draggable row; a collection spotlight; occasion edits that slide sideways while you scroll down.

Craft and store

The craft in four steps (loom, zari border, handwork, painting) with pictures that change as you read; the store; new-arrivals sign-up; a large wordmark footer.

How to review

Four things to try

Buy a saree

Home → a collection → a saree → Add to bag → Checkout → sign in (any 10 digits) → address → Pay → order placed.

Find one fast

Search “applique” or a SKU. On All sarees, filter by craft and price, sort, and switch 2 / 3 / 4 per row.

Track an order

Turn on “Signed in” in Design options, open My orders, then the shipped order: timeline, DTDC number, invoice.

Check the phone

Open Device preview or make the browser narrow: menu, filters and bag slide in from the side.

Every screen

Screens by journey

Each opens in the design you picked. Screen states (loading, empty, error, success, warning, pop-up) are in Design options.

Data fixes and what is sample

Live data to fix before launch

    Sample, draft or planned in this mock-up

    • Sample customer Priya Sharma, her two addresses and three orders. The sarees in the orders are real.
    • Payment is a stand-in for Razorpay’s window, clearly labelled. Sign-in accepts any 10-digit mobile; the SMS code is 123456.
    • Draft text to confirm with the shop: the brand lines on the home page, our story and the craft steps, returns and exchanges, saree care, delivery days (4 to 7).
    • Photos: the home page uses the shop’s own website photos (the weaver, zari borders, styled shots). A real photo shoot with models and the store would lift it further.
    • Planned, not on the website today: wishlist, “tell me when it’s back”, newsletter, colour and fabric filters (they need those details filled in).
    • Close-ups on the saree page are zoomed views of the same photo: each saree has one photo today.
    • Mock-up size: 6 sarees from each collection (355 of 1,407). Counts and totals are the live ones.
    New compared with today’s site

    What the new website adds

    • Banner carousel, shop by craft, collection spotlight, sets, recently viewed, the Mangalagiri story and the store on the home page.
    • Search that suggests collections and sarees while you type, and finds a SKU.
    • Filters (category, craft, collection, price, sets), sort, 2 / 3 / 4 per row, Load more.
    • Sets: a design with several identical pieces shows “Only N left” and lets you buy more than one.
    • Saree page with close-ups and zoom, delivery check by pincode, care and delivery details, more from the same collection.
    • Bag as a side panel and a page; a 3-step checkout with saved addresses.
    • Order page with a timeline, the DTDC number and the GST invoice; help on WhatsApp everywhere.
    • Light and dark versions, phone-first layouts, and motion that turns off for people who prefer less.
