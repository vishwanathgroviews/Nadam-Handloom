My orders

After paying, the customer can always find the order: where it is (being packed, shipped, delivered), the parcel's tracking number, the GST invoice, buy the same sarees again, and help on WhatsApp. Also every other ending: waiting for payment, confirming, refunded, cancelled by the shop, not found. Built in the live website's own look, with real sarees and prices.

How to use it

  1. Open My orders: Priya (a sample customer) has 6 orders in different states. Press View order on any of them.
  2. Try the buttons: Copy and Track on DTDC, Invoice, Buy again (it really fills the bag), Help on WhatsApp.
  3. Press Design options (bottom left) to try the lettered choices; A is my recommendation. Screen states there jumps to every state.
  4. Press Copy my choices and send them. Nothing is built before your picks.

What the old website got wrong (fixed in W7)

  • Only the first 10 orders ever showed. Now 20 at a time, with Show more.
  • Checkouts never paid were listed as "Payment Pending" forever. Now only paid orders, and one being paid while its sarees are held.
  • The order showed the address saved today, not the one it went to. Now always the address kept on the order.
  • The invoice was a public link anyone could open, and the payment signature reached the browser. Now the invoice comes through our server, only to its customer.
  • Online orders never showed "Delivered", and customers got no notice when shipped. Now both.
  • A failed load said "No orders yet". Now "Your orders didn't load. Try again".

Found while planning

  • A W6 slip: an order paid and then cancelled by the shop says "Nothing was charged". W7 fixes it: "Cancelled · refund on its way".
  • No "packed" status exists in the shop's system: "Packed" means "being packed" until it ships.
  • DTDC has no link per tracking number: "Track on DTDC" opens its page and Copy puts the number on the clipboard.
