How to review

Four things to try

Open the Design options panel (bottom right) at any time. The sales history is sample, made up the same way every time.

Read the month

Open Analytics: this month against the same days of last month. Tap a bar in the chart.

Change the view

Pick 7 days, Last month or Any dates; then Counter, WhatsApp or Online.

Look closer

Best sellers → See all; Bargains; tap a person; scroll to Stock.

Compare

Switch "Layout" (one page / tabs) and "Chart" (bars / line). Open "The first weeks" for today's real situation.
