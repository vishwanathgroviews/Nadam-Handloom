How to review

Four things to try

Open the Design options panel (bottom right) at any time. Your changes stay in this browser until you press Reset demo.

Read the day

Open Home: today's sales by Counter, WhatsApp and Online, yesterday and the month. Tap the card for the list.

Hide the amounts

Tap the eye at the top. Every ₹ turns into "₹ ••••" until you tap it again.

Act on a line

Tap "5 designs sold out, 2 running low". Press "Pretend a sale on another phone" in Design options and watch Home update.

Compare

Switch to Ravi · Staff: no money totals. Try "Home layout" and "Sales card" A / B.

Fixed from the old app

Behaviour fixes

  • Shortcuts stay still (no carousel moving every 3 seconds).
  • Orders to ship show a number; the oldest wait is in amber.
  • Recent sales newest first, not by amount.
  • "Today" is the India day, whatever the server's clock.
  • Low stock is shown (it was counted, never shown).
  • Unfinished bills, uploads and unpaid checkouts are on Home.
