Decided 11 Oct

What this mock-up follows

  • Two fixed roles: Owner and Staff. No per-person switches.
  • Owners can make other people Owners.
  • One main owner: nobody can remove them or make them Staff. Only they can hand the title over.
  • Invites are shared on WhatsApp from the owner's phone, at no cost. "Share another way" covers everyone else.
  • Staff never see money totals, analytics, the team, the activity log or settings.
  • Staff can view, add, edit and delete products, handle orders and shipping, sell and share invoices, and view categories and prices.
  • Removing someone signs them out everywhere at once and keeps their history. "Invite again" brings them back.
  • A mobile number can't be edited: remove the person and invite the new number.
Fixed from the old app

Content and behaviour fixes

  • The old invite screen promised an SMS that was never sent. Now the owner shares it on WhatsApp.
  • Only Staff could be invited, yet an error said "make someone else an owner first". Owners can now be added.
  • Staff could open the team screen and got an error. Now they never see it, and the server refuses it too.
  • Today's sales showed for everyone. Now only owners see money totals.
  • Owners couldn't see or sign out a staff member's phones. Now they can, one or all.
  • A role change now applies straight away, without signing the person out.

Not in this module

  • The screens behind each permission (Products, Orders, Analytics…): their own modules.
  • The Activity log screen that lists team changes: Module 10.
